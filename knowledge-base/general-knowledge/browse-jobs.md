# Browse Jobs

**Route:** `/jobs/browse`

**Access:** Signed-in users.

Browse Jobs lists open project requests from the backend. Users can search titles and descriptions, filter by category, budget, work type, or date, and change the result ordering. Pagination is available for larger result sets.

Each card can show details such as title, category, skills, budget, timing, visibility badges, and promotional or recommendation labels when applicable. Opening a card goes to Job Details.

Freelancers can save or unsave jobs and apply to eligible open jobs. Saving is restricted to freelancer accounts. Empty results mean the current search or filters found no eligible jobs.
