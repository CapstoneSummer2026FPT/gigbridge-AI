# Dispute Details

**Route:** `/contracts/:contractId/disputes/:disputeId`

**Access:** Authorized contract participants with completed setup.

Dispute Details shows the current case information for a contract issue. Participants can review the reason, status, involved milestone or contract context, submitted evidence, case messages, and administrator evidence requests when present.

Users can upload permitted evidence and respond to requests directed to their side. The page tracks submitted items and the current resolution state. Opening a dispute does not guarantee a particular refund or payout; the final handling comes from the platform's dispute process.
