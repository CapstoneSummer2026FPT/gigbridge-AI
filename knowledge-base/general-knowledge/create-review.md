# Create Review

**Route:** `/reviews/create`

**Access:** Clients and freelancers with completed setup and eligible completed work context.

The review form identifies the project and the party being reviewed. The user supplies the required rating information and optional written feedback according to the form rules.

Reviews are tied to completed platform work and cannot be created as anonymous, unrelated testimonials. After a successful submission, the review becomes part of the relevant profile/review history subject to moderation rules.
