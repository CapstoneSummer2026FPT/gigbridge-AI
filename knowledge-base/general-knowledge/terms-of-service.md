# Terms of Service

**Route:** `/terms`

**Access:** Public.

The Terms of Service route uses GigBridge's policy viewer. It retrieves the current published policy document from the backend and renders the returned Markdown. Because the content is server-managed, the page itself is the authoritative place to read the current version.

If loading fails, the user can retry the request. Registration separately requires explicit acceptance of the policy version presented during signup.
