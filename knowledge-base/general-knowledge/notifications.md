# Notifications

**Route:** `/notifications`

**Access:** Signed-in users.

Notifications displays account activity delivered by GigBridge. Users can filter the list, distinguish unread items, open a notification's related destination when available, mark individual items as read, and mark all notifications as read.

The page retrieves account-specific data. If there are no items, it shows an empty inbox; new proposal, message, project, contract, wallet, or administrative activity can appear when emitted by the platform.
