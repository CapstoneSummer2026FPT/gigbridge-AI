# My Jobs

**Route:** `/jobs/my-jobs`

**Access:** Clients with a completed profile.

My Jobs is the client's list of project requests. It shows current job records and their statuses and provides entry points to view, edit, or continue managing a selected job.

From this area, a client can create a new project request, open job details, manage screening questions, review related proposals, configure supported interview actions, and access job promotion features when entitled. The exact actions depend on the job's current status and platform rules.
