# Job Contract Setup

**Route:** `/jobs/post/contract`

**Access:** Clients with a completed profile and the required job-post state.

This step lets a client prepare the contract configuration associated with a project request. It uses the current job and milestone context and guides the client through the contract information required by the platform.

Contract setup is separate from funding: configuring or creating the contract does not by itself release or escrow GigCoin. Electronic-signature configuration continues on the Job E-Sign Setup page when required.
