# Freelancer Profile Promotions

**Route:** `/premium/freelancer/promotions`

**Access:** Freelancers with active Premium access.

The Promotions tab lets an entitled freelancer review available promotion packages, configure supported profile artwork/content, preview the placement, purchase with GigCoin, and inspect the active campaign.

Promotion availability, duration, price, and eligibility come from the backend. Purchasing a promotion is separate from purchasing Premium itself and requires sufficient spendable GigCoin.
