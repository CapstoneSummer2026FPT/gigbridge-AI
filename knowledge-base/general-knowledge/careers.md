# Careers at GigBridge

Join us in building the future of collaborative freelance work. We are seeking talented individuals who are passionate about design, software engineering, and AI integration.

## Why Join GigBridge?

We are on a mission to build a trusted freelance marketplace connecting elite tech talent with innovative businesses. Our team is focused on building seamless escrow integrations, smart matching layers, and interactive client-freelancer tools.

- **Impact**: Build tools that help businesses hire talent securely and freelancers guarantee payouts.
- **Modern Stack**: Work with modern frontend applications, robust .NET APIs, and Python-based AI microservices.
- **Startup Growth**: Join a fast-growing, collaborative, remote-first team.

## Benefits & Perks

- **Healthcare**: Comprehensive medical and dental insurance plans.
- **Compensation**: Competitive salary packages.
- **Flexibility**: Flexible work hours and remote-first setup.
- **Equipment**: Premium workspace equipment setup.
- **Learning**: Dedicated annual learning and professional development resources.

## Open Departments

- **Software Engineering**: Building secure payment gateways, e-sign canvases, and responsive user dashboards.
- **Product Design**: Designing simple and premium experiences for clients and freelancers.
- **AI & Machine Learning**: Improving RAG matching systems and interactive mock voice interview services.
- **Security & Operations**: Keeping user wallets secure and moderating dispute resolutions.

For open position inquiries or application submittals, contact our hiring team at `careers@gigbridge.com`.
