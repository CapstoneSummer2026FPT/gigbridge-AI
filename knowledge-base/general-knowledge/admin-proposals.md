# Admin Proposals Audit

The Admin Proposals Audit page provides administrators with auditing capabilities over freelancer proposals submitted across all active job postings.

---

## 1. Page Access & Routes

- **Routes**: `/admin/proposals`, `/admin/proposals/:proposalId`
- **Access**: Restricted to `Admin` role.

---

## 2. Proposal Directory & Filters

- **Search**: Search by Proposal ID, Freelancer Name, Client Name, or Job Title.
- **Status Filter**: `Pending`, `Shortlisted`, `Hired`, `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`.
- **AI Assist Indicator**: Filter proposals generated or enhanced with AI cover letter tools.

---

## 3. Proposal Detail Inspection

- **Proposed Pricing & Timeline**: Proposed milestone budget breakdown and estimated completion days.
- **Cover Letter & Deliverable Attachments**: Full bid text and attached portfolio files.
- **Screening Question Answers**: Submitted responses to job screening questions.
- **Freelancer Historical Stats**: Freelancer Elo score, completion rate, and active contract count at time of bidding.
