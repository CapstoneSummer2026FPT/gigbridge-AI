# Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using the GigBridge freelance marketplace platform.

## Getting Started

### What is GigBridge?

GigBridge is a secure, AI-powered freelance marketplace designed for tech professionals and clients in Vietnam. It integrates automated screening tools, digital e-signed contract templates, and milestone-based escrow payments into a single, unified workflow to establish trust and efficiency.

### How do I create an account?

Go to the Sign Up page, select your target role (Freelancer or Client), enter your email, full name, phone number, and password, and complete the email verification step. Once registered, you will be guided through the interactive Profile Setup wizard.

### Can I use GigBridge before completing my profile?

No. To apply for jobs or post jobs, users must complete the interactive profile setup to ensure safety and maintain quality standards within the platform.

### Is there a GigBridge mobile app?

No. GigBridge is a web-only platform. There is no native mobile application (iOS or Android) available on any app store. All client and freelancer features are fully accessible and mobile-optimized via web browsers at https://gigbridge.id.vn.

## For Clients

### How do I post a job?

1. Log in to your Client account and navigate to the "My Jobs" page.
2. Click "Create New Job Post".
3. Follow the multi-step form to outline the job title, category, budget (Min/Max in VND/G-coin), estimated duration, required skills, and any optional screening questions.
4. Set up the contract template and E-Sign requirement parameters.
5. Click Publish to make the job visible on the public board and trigger the AI Match recommendation system.

### Can I edit a job after posting it?

Yes. Navigate to the job post details screen, click the "Edit" button, make the necessary adjustments, and save. However, any active contract negotiations or signed contracts associated with the previous version of the job will remain unchanged to protect both parties.

## For Freelancers

### How do I apply for a job?

Navigate to the "Browse Jobs" page, search by keyword or filter by skills, select a job post to view its detailed requirements, and click "Apply". If the client has screening questions, you must answer them before submitting your bid (cover letter, proposed rate, and estimated timeline).

### How can I improve my chances of getting hired?

- Complete your profile details (experience, bio, portfolio links) to achieve a high Profile Completion Score.
- Take skill tests and assessments to verify your expertise.
- Respond to messages and invitations within 24 hours to maintain high responsiveness ratings.
- Utilize the AI Proposal Generator (Premium feature) to draft highly tailored, relevant cover letters.

### Can I save jobs to apply later?

Yes. Click the bookmark icon on any job card in the Browse Jobs view to add it to your "Saved Jobs" list.

## Payments & Billing

### How are payments handled?

GigBridge uses a milestone-based escrow wallet system to secure payments. The client funds each milestone when it is created. The funds are held in escrow by the platform and are only released to the freelancer's wallet balance after the client reviews and approves the deliverable.

### When does a freelancer get paid?

A freelancer gets paid immediately when the client approves the milestone deliverable. If a client is unresponsive, the freelancer can submit proof of work, which will be audited by administrators or automatically released after a system-defined review window.

### Are there platform commission fees?

Yes. GigBridge charges a **10% platform commission fee** on all completed contract payments. This commission is deducted automatically from the milestone budget upon release.

### What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum amount you can withdraw from your GigBridge wallet is **50,000 VND**. Payouts are reviewed and approved manually by platform administrators.

### What are the subscription plans?

- **Freelancer Basic**: Free, limits applications to 10 proposals per month, standard profile visibility.
- **Freelancer Premium Monthly**: 150 GIG coins per month, unlimited proposals, premium profile badge, rank protection, profile promotion features.
- **Freelancer Premium Yearly**: 1500 GIG coins per year (saves 300 GIG coins/year).
- **Client Basic**: Free, limits job postings to 3 per month.
- **Client Premium**: 500,000 VND per month, unlimited job postings, AI candidate filtering, smart recommendations, 24/7 support.

## Contracts & Disputes

### What is a contract on GigBridge?

A contract is a legally binding electronic agreement between a client and a freelancer specifying deliverables, project scope, payment milestones, and terms. Contracts are generated automatically based on standard templates.

### Can contracts be signed electronically?

Yes. GigBridge features a built-in E-Sign Signature Workflow. Both parties sign the documents using an interactive canvas drawing pad directly on their respective screens before the contract goes active.

### What should I do if there is a dispute?

If a disagreement arises regarding milestone deliverables or payment releases:

1. Either party can initiate a dispute from the contract details page.
2. The milestone funds are locked in escrow.
3. Platform administrators review the workspace communication records, task status, and submitted deliverables to mediate and issue a final payout split.
