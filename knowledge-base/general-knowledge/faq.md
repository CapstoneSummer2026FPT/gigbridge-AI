# Frequently Asked Questions

**Route:** `/faq`

**Access:** Public.

The FAQ page loads active question categories and answers from the GigBridge backend. Users can search question/answer text, filter by category, search category names/slugs, and expand individual answers.

FAQ content is managed by the platform and can change without a frontend release. If categories or answers cannot be loaded, the page displays an error instead of fallback answers.

For stable workflow questions, the page-specific knowledge documents in this collection explain current routes and actions, including signup, profiles, jobs, proposals, contracts, milestones, wallet top-ups, withdrawals, Premium, AI interviews, and talent matching. Current prices, limits, statuses, and policy text should be taken from the relevant live page/API response rather than old hard-coded FAQ values.
