# Talent Matching

**Route:** `/talent-matching`

**Access:** Clients with a completed profile.

The page has three related discovery modes:

* **Browse:** Search and filter the freelancer directory by available profile attributes.
* **Saved:** Review freelancers previously saved by the client.
* **Smart matching:** Select an open job and request a ranked shortlist of eligible freelancers.

Smart matching requires active Client Premium and an open job. Results include transparent matching information based on skills, category/relevance, track record, and platform activity signals. Clients can open profiles, save or unsave freelancers, and send eligible job invitations.

No-result states distinguish between missing open jobs, unavailable Premium access, loading failures, and filters that match no freelancers.
