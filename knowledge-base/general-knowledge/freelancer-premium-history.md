# Freelancer Premium History

**Route:** `/premium/freelancer/history`

**Access:** Freelancers with active Premium access.

The History tab shows subscription records with plan names, coverage dates, and statuses. It also shows wallet transactions related to subscription and promotion purchases.

This page is focused on Premium activity. The full wallet ledger, including other transaction types, is available on Wallet History.
