# Freelancer Premium Elo Points

**Route:** `/premium/freelancer/points`

**Access:** Freelancers with active Premium access.

The Points tab shows the freelancer's current Elo points, tier name, progress toward the next tier, and recent point changes returned by the platform. Positive and negative changes include their recorded activity/reason and timestamp.

If the freelancer has reached the highest configured tier, the page reports that instead of a next-tier target. Elo points are reputation/progression data and are separate from spendable wallet GigCoin.
