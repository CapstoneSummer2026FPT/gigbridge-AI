# Contracts

**Route:** `/contracts`

**Access:** Clients and freelancers with completed setup.

The route renders a role-appropriate contract list. Clients see the contracts they manage with freelancers; freelancers see contracts for their accepted work. Records reflect live statuses rather than a static list.

Users can open a contract to review its parties, documents, milestones, funding, deliverables, signing state, and disputes when available. Actions such as signing, funding, submitting, approving, or reporting an issue depend on role and contract status.
