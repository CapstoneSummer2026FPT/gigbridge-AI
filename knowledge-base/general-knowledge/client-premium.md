# Client Premium Hub

The Client Premium Hub shows subscription status and links Premium hiring capabilities to the screens where they are actually used. Standard clients can preview the same tools, but protected actions route them to pricing until a valid entitlement exists.

---

## 1. Entitlement & Plan Status

- **Route**: `/premium/client`
- **Active condition**: The current subscription is Premium, has active status, and has an end date later than now.
- **Status card**: Shows the plan name and remaining covered time assembled from current/history records.
- **History**: Lists plan name, start/end dates, and subscription status.

A purchase-return notice is shown when arriving from successful pricing checkout.

---

## 2. Premium Hiring Tools

- **Smart talent matching** ranks eligible freelancers using skills, category, reputation, and availability signals.
- **AI job builder** turns a brief into a draft that the client must review before publishing.
- **Promoted jobs** use GigCoin to feature an open listing.
- **AI interview setup** attaches structured text or voice screening to an open job.
- **Priority dispute handling** places eligible client disputes into the Premium priority workflow with AI-assisted analysis.

Buttons open Talent Matching, job creation, My Jobs, Contracts, or the promotion studio when entitled; otherwise they open pricing.

---

## 3. Renewal Controls

An active client can toggle automatic renewal. When enabled, the plan renews using GigCoin. `Cancel renewal` requires confirmation and stops the future renewal while keeping benefits through the current end date. Every successful change broadcasts a Premium-updated event and refreshes current/history data.

---

## 4. Job Promotion Studio

The hub includes a dedicated studio for open-job promotion. Clients select an eligible job, design/crop artwork, preview the final card, purchase a campaign with GigCoin, and inspect active or historical campaigns. Premium entitlement gates the purchase action; it does not hide the explanatory interface from Standard clients.
