# Client Premium Hub

**Route:** `/premium/client`

**Access:** Clients with completed setup.

The hub shows whether Client Premium is active, the plan and remaining coverage, automatic renewal, and subscription history. Standard clients can preview Premium capabilities and go to pricing; entitled clients can open the tools.

Premium capabilities shown by the site include Smart Talent Matching, AI-assisted job drafting, promoted job posts, AI interview setup, and priority dispute handling. The page also contains the job-promotion studio for configuring, previewing, purchasing, and tracking promotions for eligible open jobs.

Cancelling renewal leaves existing Premium benefits active through the current end date.
