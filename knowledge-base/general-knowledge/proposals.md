# Proposals Inbox

**Route:** `/proposals`

**Access:** Clients and freelancers with completed setup.

The Proposals page changes by role. Freelancers see proposals they submitted and can track their current states. Clients see proposals received for their project requests and can inspect candidates, answers, pricing, milestone plans, and the actions allowed by each proposal state.

Proposal actions may include opening details, editing an eligible submission, reviewing screening answers, starting or continuing a conversation, and progressing toward negotiation or contract creation. Availability depends on ownership and current backend status.
