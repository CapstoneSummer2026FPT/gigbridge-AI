# Freelancer Dashboard

**Route:** `/freelancer/dashboard`

**Access:** Freelancers with a completed profile.

The freelancer dashboard is the starting point for finding work and tracking activity. It summarizes current opportunities and account progress from live platform data, including proposals, invitations, contracts or projects, earnings, and profile-related information when available.

The dashboard links into Browse Jobs, Saved Jobs, Job Invitations, Proposals, Contracts, Projects, Messages, Reviews, Wallet, and profile or Premium actions. Values are account-specific; they are not fixed platform-wide statistics.
