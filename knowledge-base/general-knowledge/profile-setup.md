# Profile Setup

**Route:** `/onboarding/profile-setup`

**Access:** Signed-in client or freelancer accounts that have not finished setup.

Profile Setup is a two-step, role-specific onboarding flow.

## Client setup

Clients provide company information such as company name, website, company size, industry, location, and company description. Company name and industry are required to leave the first step, and location is required to finish.

## Freelancer setup

Freelancers provide a professional title, major, one or more categories, availability, location, and biography. Major and category options are loaded from the platform taxonomy. A title, major, category, location, and bio are required to finish.

Successful completion marks the account as set up and sends the user to the client or freelancer dashboard.
