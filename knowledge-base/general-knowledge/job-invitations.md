# Job Invitations

**Route:** `/jobs/invitations`

**Access:** Signed-in users with completed setup; the invitation inbox is a freelancer workflow.

This page lists direct invitations sent by clients to a freelancer. Each invitation is tied to a job and carries its current invitation status and available response actions.

The freelancer can inspect the related job and respond when the invitation is still actionable. Accepting or opening an invitation does not itself create a contract; the proposal, negotiation, and contract workflows still apply.
