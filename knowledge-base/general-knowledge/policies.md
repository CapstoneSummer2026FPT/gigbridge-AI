# GigBridge Policy

**Route:** `/policies`

**Access:** Public.

The Policy page retrieves the current published GigBridge Vietnam policy from the platform API and renders its Markdown content. The policy text is controlled by the backend and may be updated independently of the frontend.

If the policy cannot be loaded, the page shows an error and a Retry action. Users should rely on the policy version displayed on the page when registering or reviewing platform rules.
