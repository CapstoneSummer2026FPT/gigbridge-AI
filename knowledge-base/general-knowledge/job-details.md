# Job Details

**Routes:** `/jobs/:id`, `/jobs/my-jobs/:jobPostId`

**Access:** Signed-in users.

Job Details displays the current project request returned by the backend, including its title, client context, description, category, helpful skills, budget, schedule, status, proposal information, interview requirements, and baseline milestones when present.

Eligible freelancers can start a proposal and may be offered an apply-with-interview path when the job uses AI interview questions. The owning client can access management actions such as editing the job or reviewing its hiring activity.

Available buttons depend on role, ownership, job status, deadlines, existing applications, and subscription rules.
