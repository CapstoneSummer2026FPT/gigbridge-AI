# Answer Proposal Questions

**Route:** `/proposals/create/:jobPostId/questions`

**Access:** Freelancers with completed setup who are applying to the job.

This application step shows the screening questions configured by the client. Required questions must have an answer before the proposal can continue; optional questions may be left unanswered.

Answers are associated with the proposal and can later be reviewed by the client. The page handles missing job/question state and prevents submission when required responses are incomplete.
