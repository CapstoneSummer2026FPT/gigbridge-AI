# Freelancer Premium Pricing

**Route:** `/premium/freelancer/pricing`

**Access:** Freelancers with completed setup.

Pricing compares the free freelancer experience with Premium plans published by the backend. Premium can include Elo tiers, rank protection, Premium identity, and profile promotion. The page shows the user's current spendable GigCoin balance.

The current monthly plan is loaded from the platform. A yearly option may be derived from or supplied alongside that plan. The confirmation dialog shows plan price, current balance, and projected balance after purchase.

If the balance is insufficient, the user is directed to Wallet Deposit. A successful purchase activates or extends Premium and opens the Premium hub.
