# Post a Job Guide

**Route:** `/jobs/post/guide`

**Access:** Clients with a completed profile.

This page introduces the job-posting workflow before the form begins. It explains what information the client should prepare and offers the available path into creating a project request.

Clients can continue with standard manual entry. When Client Premium access is active, they can also enable Instant Job Detail and describe the requirement in a prompt; AI then proposes a title, category, description, and skills for the client to review. AI output is a draft and remains editable before publication.
