# E-Sign Contracts

**Route:** `/contracts/esign`

**Access:** Clients and freelancers with completed setup.

This page lists electronic contract records or documents available to the signed-in user. It helps participants find agreements that need attention and inspect their current signing state.

Opening an eligible item leads to its contract or signing workflow. A listed document is not necessarily active or fully signed; the current participant signatures and contract state determine what happens next.
