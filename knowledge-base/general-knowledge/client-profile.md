# Client Profile

**Route:** `/profile/client/:id`

**Access:** Signed-in users.

A client profile shows the account identity and company information available to other platform participants. This can include company name, location, website, industry, company size, description, Elo points, job or project history, and reviews from freelancers.

The profile owner can open Settings to edit company details. Other users may copy the profile link or submit a user report. Review lists are paginated when multiple reviews are available.

The page displays only information returned for that client; missing profile fields may appear empty.
