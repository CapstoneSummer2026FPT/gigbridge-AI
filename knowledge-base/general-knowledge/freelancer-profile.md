# Freelancer Profile

**Route:** `/profile/freelancer/:id`

**Access:** Signed-in users.

A freelancer profile presents the freelancer's identity and professional evidence. Depending on available data, it shows the professional title, location, availability, biography, major and categories, skills, Elo points or tier, recent work, portfolio projects, work experience, and client reviews.

The profile owner can open Settings to edit the profile. Other users may share the profile or report it. Eligible clients can invite the freelancer to a job, subject to the current job and invitation rules.

Profile sections may be empty when the freelancer has not supplied that information.
