# Contract Signature Workflow

**Route:** `/contracts/:contractId/sign`

**Access:** Authorized contract participants with completed setup.

This workflow guides a participant through the contract's signing requirements. It shows the relevant agreement/document status and directs the user to any document that still requires their signature.

Signing is participant-specific. The contract advances only when the required server-side signing conditions are satisfied; one party completing a signature does not imply the other party has signed or that escrow is funded.
