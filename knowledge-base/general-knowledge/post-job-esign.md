# Job E-Sign Setup

**Routes:** `/jobs/post/esign`, `/jobs/post/contract/esign`

**Access:** Clients with a completed profile and the required job or contract state.

This page configures the electronic-signature portion of a project-request contract. It lets the client review the relevant contract context and prepare the document/signing requirements used later by the parties.

The page does not represent a completed signature. Actual participant signing occurs after a contract and document exist, through the contract signature workflow and document signing pad.
