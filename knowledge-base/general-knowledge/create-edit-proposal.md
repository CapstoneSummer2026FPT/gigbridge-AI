# Create or Edit Proposal

**Routes:** `/proposals/create/:jobPostId`, `/proposals/:proposalId/edit`

**Access:** Freelancers with completed setup and an eligible job or proposal.

The proposal form lets a freelancer respond to a job with a cover letter or message, proposed project terms, and a milestone plan. The detailed task breakdown can expand on the client's baseline milestones while preserving the relationship between scope and payment.

The page validates totals and required information before submission. Editing is available only while the proposal is in a state the platform permits to change.

If the job has screening questions, the freelancer is directed to answer them. If the client enabled an AI interview, the submitted proposal may offer a Start or Continue AI Interview action.
