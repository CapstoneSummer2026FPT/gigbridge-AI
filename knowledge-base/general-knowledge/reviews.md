# My Reviews

**Route:** `/reviews`

**Access:** Clients and freelancers with completed setup.

My Reviews separates feedback into **Received** and **Sent** sections. Entries show the related project and participant context, rating, comment when supplied, and moderation visibility/status.

Lists support pagination. A user can report a problematic received review by selecting an issue type and providing sufficient details. Reporting submits the review for moderation; it does not immediately delete or rewrite the review.
