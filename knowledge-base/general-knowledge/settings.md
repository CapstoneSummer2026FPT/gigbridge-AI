# Account Settings

**Route:** `/settings`

**Access:** Signed-in users.

Settings contains four sections:

* **General:** Update avatar and basic account information plus the detailed freelancer or client profile. Freelancer settings include professional taxonomy and bio fields; client settings include company fields.
* **Security:** Change the account password by providing the current password and confirming the new one.
* **Payment:** Manage role-appropriate payment or bank-account information and use shortcuts to wallet actions.
* **Preferences:** Change supported language, theme, and available account preferences.

Profile edit routes redirect here. Changes are not final until the relevant Save or Update action succeeds.
