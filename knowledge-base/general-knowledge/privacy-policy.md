# Privacy Policy

**Route:** `/privacy`

**Access:** Public.

The Privacy route uses GigBridge's policy viewer and loads the currently published policy document from the backend. The returned Markdown is displayed with headings, lists, tables, links, and other policy formatting.

Policy content can change without a frontend release, so users should consult this route for the current published text. A Retry action is available if the policy service cannot be reached.
