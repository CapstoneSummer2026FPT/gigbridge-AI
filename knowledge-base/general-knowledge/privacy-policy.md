# Privacy Policy

The Privacy route exposes the platform's current policy document through the same public Policy viewer used by the Terms and Policies routes. Signup links here so users can review the available privacy-related provisions before consenting.

---

## 1. Page Access & Source

- **Route**: `/privacy`
- **Access**: Public.
- **Content Source**: GigBridge Vietnam policy Markdown requested from the backend.
- **Signup Link**: Opens in a new browser tab from the registration consent section.

The actual server-delivered document is authoritative for what appears on this route at the time it is viewed.

---

## 2. Viewer Behavior

- Markdown headings, paragraphs, lists, tables, blockquotes, links, and separators are supported.
- External links open in a separate tab with safe browser attributes.
- The article adapts to desktop and mobile widths, including horizontal scrolling for large tables.
- The route does not expose account editing or privacy-request submission controls inside the policy viewer itself.

---

## 3. Loading, Failure & Retry

1. The page displays a loading status while retrieving the policy.
2. On success, it renders the returned text.
3. On failure, it shows an error and a Retry action.
4. Retry requests the policy again without changing account information.

---

## 4. Consent & Account Actions

Viewing this page does not automatically accept the policy. Registration acceptance is recorded through the Signup form's required consent control. Authenticated profile, security, payment, and preference changes are handled separately in Settings.
