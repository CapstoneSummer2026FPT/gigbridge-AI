# Manage Contract Milestones

**Route:** `/contracts/:contractId/milestones`

**Access:** Client or freelancer participant in the contract.

The milestone page shows each agreed work stage, including its scope, amount, timing, deliverables, acceptance criteria, and current status. The exact controls differ by role.

Clients can perform permitted funding and review actions. Freelancers can open eligible milestones to submit deliverables. Both parties can inspect progress and supported contract-change information.

GigCoin moves according to backend wallet and escrow state. A planned milestone is not funded until the platform confirms the funding transaction, and a submission is not paid until the applicable approval/release rules succeed.
