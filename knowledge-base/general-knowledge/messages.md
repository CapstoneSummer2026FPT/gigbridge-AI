# Messages

**Route:** `/messages`

**Access:** Clients and freelancers with completed setup.

Messages is the conversation inbox for client-freelancer communication. Users can select a conversation, exchange messages, and receive updated conversation data through the platform's real-time/polling behavior.

Supported conversations can progress into negotiation. Participants can discuss and respond to structured offers and milestone plans, with the interface showing whether an offer is old, pending, agreed, declined, or syncing.

Users can also create or edit schedules in Vietnam Time (ICT). When a Google account is connected, a schedule may include a Google Meet room. Blocking/unblocking and other conversation actions appear only when allowed by the current relationship and status.
