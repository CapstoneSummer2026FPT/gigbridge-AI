# Sign Up

**Route:** `/auth/signup`

**Access:** Guests only.

Registration begins by choosing either the **Client** or **Freelancer** role. The selected role controls the profile and workflows available after signup and is not treated as an interchangeable account mode.

Users may register with Google or with their full name, email address, password, and password confirmation. Email registration includes an OTP verification step. The user must accept the current GigBridge policy before the account can be created.

After successful registration, the user signs in and completes the role-specific Profile Setup flow. Existing users can return to Login.
